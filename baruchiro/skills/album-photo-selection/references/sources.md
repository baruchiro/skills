# Sources

Read on 2026-11-19. All are professional practice, interviews or vendor guides. None is controlled research. Vendor guides are commercial and their dimensions do not apply to other printers. Numbers match the references in [guidance](guidance.md).

1. Gregory Halpern, Magnum: Editing and Sequencing. https://www.magnumphotos.com/theory-and-practice/gregory-halpern-editing-and-sequencing/ . Photographer, 2022. Relationships between photos can matter more than each photo alone: pairs, contrast, time.
2. Stuart Smith, Aperture: How Not to Design a Photobook. https://aperture.org/editorial/design-photobook/ . Interview with a photobook designer, 2016. A tight edit plus a wide pool of alternates, since pacing and pairing need options. His reservations about print on demand concern art books, not family albums.
3. Queensberry album design tips. https://queensberrynz.kayako.com/article/426-queensberry-s-album-design-tips . Professional album maker. Thinking in spreads, lead images, size that shows expressions, chronology with context, care with the fold. Trim dimensions there are not a universal rule.
4. Dan Milnor, Blurb: How to Self-Edit. https://www.blurb.com/blog/hit-the-books-with-dan-milnor-how-to-self-edit-photography/ . Photographer and print-provider voice. One scene, one photo as a narrowing exercise; check expression, light, background; choose a cover by role. A demanding editing exercise, not a family quota.
5. Wonderful Machine: Producing a Photobook. https://wonderfulmachine.com/article/expert-advice-self-publishing-a-photobook/ . Matching photos, reducing repetition, size changes, pauses, trying sequences. Commercial or art context.
6. Aperture: How to Produce a Photobook. https://aperture.org/editorial/how-to-produce-a-photobook/ . Interviews with four book people. Size and binding change reading; a dummy is not the same as viewing a file. Industrial print limits do not carry over to consumer albums.
7. Blurb: How to Make a Great Photo Book. https://www.blurb.com/blog/make-great-photo-book/ . Provider guide. Start from key moments, organise, sequence, stay consistent, captions, spreads.
8. Kelly Tareski: Building a Balanced Family Album. https://kellytareskiphotography.com/from-poses-to-candid-shots-building-a-balanced-family-album/ . Family photographer's blog. People and relationships, portrait next to candid, when a change in interaction justifies another photo. Professional marketing source.
9. Artifact Uprising: Photo Book Building Guide. https://www.artifactuprising.com/diy/photo-book-building-guide . Provider documentation. Safe area, preview, resolution warning; a smaller layout can suit a photo that will not enlarge. File instructions there are for that vendor only.
10. VistaPrint: Photo Book Creation Pro Tips. https://www.vistaprint.com/hub/photobook-creation-pro-tips . Provider guide, 2025. Chronology versus themes, wide shots with close-ups, avoiding crowding. Per-page counts are a starting style, not a rule.
