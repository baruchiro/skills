# Research and evidence

1. Identify the exact voucher product, not merely its issuer. Separate redemption, membership discount and cashback.
2. Through `web_search`/`web_extract` or permitted browser tools, inspect current official issuer directories and merchant payment terms. A page shell with no actual merchant entries proves nothing. Secondary sources are leads, not confirmation.
3. Verify item restrictions, channel, branch and seller explicitly. Store logos do not establish all-product eligibility; online listings do not establish same-day branch stock.
4. Record product/merchant IDs, exact scope, allow/deny, conditions, source URL, publisher, official/secondary authority, short evidence excerpt and actual observation timestamp. Use a clock tool for the timestamp. Never fabricate a check date or refresh it merely by reading a cache.
5. Preserve contradictory official evidence as separate rules; no silent newest-wins or specific-wins policy. Missing evidence is unknown, not denial. Confirm fresh official evidence before presenting acceptance as sourced.
6. Propose exact changes and obtain separate permission before editing any external catalog or wallet. Keep old evidence and backups outside Git; validate a candidate before any authorized replacement. The lookup helper itself has no update command.

No reusable merchant acceptance findings are supplied by the synthetic fixtures. Their source URLs use reserved example.invalid hosts and are not usable evidence. Do not upload operational catalog snapshots, private authorization records or personal holdings as contributions. A contribution must follow the host's approval process and contain only sanitized, verified reusable material.
